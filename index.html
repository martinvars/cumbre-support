Cumbre

Race the rider you were. Compite contra quien fuiste.

Support

Cumbre is a cycling computer for the iPhone. It records the ride on the phone: distance, speed, climb, and the road. There is no account.

On a road you have already ridden for about 3 km, Cumbre lines up your past efforts so you can see your place while you are still pedaling. A list of Spanish climbs, including the Angliru, Lagos de Covadonga, and the Veleta, is stored on the phone. A heart-rate strap, a power meter, and a rear radar are optional.

You vs You from Strava

  1. On a computer, sign in at strava.com.
  2. Open your profile photo, then Settings, then My Account.
  3. Choose Download or Delete Your Account and request your archive. Strava emails a link. It can take a few hours.
  4. Download the zip, unzip it, and find activities.csv.
  5. AirDrop that file to the iPhone, or save it in Files.
  6. In Cumbre, open You vs You, tap Import Strava Data, and choose activities.csv.

A race is a stretch of about 1 to 4.5 km, and the file needs the ride’s start point. Strava’s activities.csv usually has no start location, so those rows are skipped. Ride the road once in Cumbre for about 3 km and the next time you are there, you race that effort.

Soporte

Cumbre es un ciclocomputador para el iPhone. Registra la salida en el teléfono: distancia, velocidad, desnivel y carretera. No hay cuenta.

En una carretera que ya recorriste unos 3 km, Cumbre alinea tus esfuerzos anteriores para que veas tu puesto mientras pedaleas. Lleva en el teléfono una lista de puertos españoles, entre ellos el Angliru, los Lagos de Covadonga y el Veleta. La cinta de pulso, el medidor de potencia y el radar son opcionales.
